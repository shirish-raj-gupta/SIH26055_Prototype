### Reading note on figure of merit 6

The problem statement asks for *"percentage of correct predictions"*, and it is
reported above. It should not be read as the headline: channel occupancy is
roughly 8 % positive, so a model that predicts "idle" everywhere scores the
*always-idle* row — within a couple of points of the real model — while being
useless. Average precision collapses to the base rate for that model instead,
which is why the scheduler is selected on AP and why the lift over base rate is
quoted beside it. The scheduler ranks channels by predicted occupancy and never
applies a threshold, so accuracy, precision and recall describe an operating
point it does not use.
