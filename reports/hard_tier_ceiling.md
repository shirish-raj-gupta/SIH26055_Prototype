# Why the HARD tier resists every scheduler

_Generated by `scripts/hard_tier_ceiling.py`._

A receiver seeing `K` of `B` channels visits any given channel a fraction
`K/B` of the time, so an emitter physically catchable in `n` slots offers
`n·K/B` expected chances across the episode — for **any** policy with uniform
coverage, however clever its ordering. Below one expected chance, catching it
is a coin flip that no scheduling, training or compute converts into a
certainty.
